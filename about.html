Atelier Sareno

My journey into fashion began with painting, where colour, form, and proportion became my language of expression. Studying fashion design brought that artistic perspective to fabric and the structure of clothing.

Atelier Sareno grew from my love of this connection and of working personally with people. For me, design begins with a conversation: understanding the person who will wear the garment, their taste, and how they want to feel.

In every consultation, I bring this perspective on art and design together with carefully selected fabrics and Italian craftsmanship to create a garment that fits beautifully and reflects the individuality of its wearer.

Based in Melville, Long Island, New York, our appointment-only showroom brings together fabric selection, design consultations, measurements and fittings for men and women. Each custom garment is made in Italy.

Maryam Bahrami
Founder, Atelier Sareno
