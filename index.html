Custom suits on Long Island

Atelier Sareno creates custom-made suits for men and women, custom shirts and occasion wear. Meet personally with founder and designer Maryam Bahrami for fabric selection, design, measurements and fittings at our Melville showroom. Your garments are then crafted in Italy.

Visit us by appointment at 734 Walt Whitman Road, Suite 202, Melville, New York 11747. Our Long Island showroom welcomes clients from nearby Huntington, Dix Hills, Syosset and across New York. Consultations run 60–90 minutes.
